♿ Assistive Technology & Special Needs Empowerment Guide

Dedicated resources, assistive controls, and implementation guidelines for speech-impaired individuals, special education teachers, and children with learning differences.

⚙️ Accessibility Preference Controls

High Contrast Dark Mode Enhances text visibility and joint node contrast for low-vision learners.
OpenDyslexic Friendly Font Switches UI typography to weighted bottom fonts to reduce character flipping.
Large Touch Targets Enlarges button padding for children and motor-impaired users.

🗣️ For Mute & Speech-Impaired Individuals

  • Real-Time Voice Translation: Converts hand gestures to spoken speech using the Web Speech Synthesis Engine.
  • Sentence Builder History: Accumulate recognized sign letters into complete sentences with one-click speech playback.
  • Text-to-Sign Diffusion Player: Type custom messages to visualize synthesized 3D landmark animations.

🧒 For Special Children & Educators

  • Visual Feedback Overlay: Real-time glowing joint nodes (wrist red, joints green, connections cyan) provide immediate motor confirmation.
  • Interactive ASL Dictionary: Step-by-step finger position instructions with audio playback for every alphabet and phrase.
  • Top-3 Probability Assist: Shows alternative sign candidates if a gesture is partially formed.

🧠 AI Architectural Overview

  • Normalized 3D Landmarks: Wrist-centered translation & scale normalization eliminate camera distance sensitivity.
  • Spatial-Temporal Transformer: Multi-Head Self-Attention model tracks 3D trajectory motion sequences over time.
  • Denoising Diffusion Synthesizer: DDPM neural network generates frame-by-frame 3D hand skeleton sequences.
